This publication explores the everyday world of London’s corner shops. Through photographs, collected materials and personal accounts, it takes readers on a visual and literary journey that leads from the streets of London ever deeper into the shops and their stories. The focus is on different perspectives, individual experiences and encounters with customers and shop owners. Although we are all in the same place, each of us perceives it differently.
