Poster Design
895 mm x 1280 mm

In collaboration with the Natural History Museum Bern and ETH Zurich, a three-dimensional sculpture exploring biodiversity was developed. Based on scientific studies, individual elements were designed and brought together to form a single object in the workshop. As part of the exhibition, 14 sculptures transform the museum garden into a sculpture park, illustrating how biodiversity, cooperation and the balance of ecosystems are interconnected.
