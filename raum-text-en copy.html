3D Design

How does two-dimensional typography become a three-dimensional object? Starting with a typeface I designed myself, I created a three-dimensional wooden model which, when illuminated in a specific way, produces fascinating patterns of light and shadow. The shadows transform the original letterforms, distorting them, making them overlap and giving rise to new, sometimes surprising structures. A project that combines craftsmanship with graphic design, shaped by improvisation and experimentation.
