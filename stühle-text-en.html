Three pieces of furniture from the Bauhaus era form the starting point for a creative journey back to the 1920s. This small brochure combines historical forms and design classics with a contemporary layout. The typography and composition draw on the clear aesthetics of the Bauhaus and translate them into a modern visual language. In this way, the past and the present come together in a compact format.
